# Code of Conduct

This project expects professional, respectful collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be clear, constructive, and specific.
- Focus feedback on code, documentation, design, and evidence.
- Assume public comments may be read by people with different backgrounds and
  levels of context.
- Avoid sharing secrets, private data, or confidential customer information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Repeated off-topic disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull
requests; or block participants when behavior is harmful to the project or its
contributors.
